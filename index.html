04 · The research question

How do we turn images into useful information?

Preoperative MRI shapes every surgical decision. My work is to extract measurable information from it and turn that into a prediction the surgeon can use before operating.

05 · Two studies, one pipeline

From data to prediction

  • Cavernous sinus invasion in pituitary adenomas

    Two-centre study · with Prof. Solari, Dr. Cuocolo and Dr. Ugga · manuscript being submitted

    I built the full analysis pipeline to predict invasion from preoperative MRI: deep learning models, testing on an external centre, and a multi-reader study.

  • Craniopharyngioma: predicting the outcome before surgery

    MD thesis · endoscopic endonasal approach

    I segmented every tumour on T2 MRI and compared a clinical model with a clinical-radiomic one, using nested cross-validation, bootstrap and decision curve analysis. Radiomics added nothing over the clinical predictors, and I reported it as a negative result.

Also: first author of three systematic review protocols registered on PROSPERO, and reviewer for European Radiology Experimental.
